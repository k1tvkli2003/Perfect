# pg-conflict-center — autonomous acceptance

- Preview: `pg-conflict-center@ps01-pages-1.0.0`
- Family: Owner tools and recovery
- Problem: How do I understand and change this owner-level setting without risking data or session continuity? Surface: Conflict center.
- Primary action: **Inspect and recover**
- Fixture: `fx-system-adversarial` (preview-only)

## Candidates compared

- **Focus Spine** (`candidate-focus-spine`): One continuous vertical answer path with the primary action inside reach, not in a detached dashboard card.
- **Split Workbench** (`candidate-split-workbench`): Master context and live work remain adjacent; wide layouts gain an inspector while compact layouts preserve route continuity.
- **Command Ledger** (`candidate-command-ledger`): Query, filters and reversible actions lead a dense evidence-first ledger with restrained decoration.

## Verdicts

- **Modernize:** The candidate-command-ledger topology reduces navigation or interpretation cost and avoids a generic card dashboard.
- **Integrity:** Brand, system state, component ownership, local-first truth and all consumers remain in one canonical language.
- **Anatomy:** Primary question and action own the scan path; scroll, sticky layers, reach zones and platform transformation are explicit.
- **Style:** Perfect pastel material, exact identity, live typography, optical alignment and restrained glass are applied by role.
- **Critics:** Sparse/dense, dark/high contrast, 200% mixed copy, short height, IME, offline/error and continuous resize are adversarial inputs, not afterthoughts.

## Selected

**Command Ledger** wins because it answers the route's primary question before exposing secondary evidence, preserves direct action continuity, and recomposes by platform without stretching a phone canvas. The exact selection is `candidate-command-ledger`.

## Strongest rejected alternative

**Split Workbench** lost: The split workbench added a second pane before this route had a second simultaneous job, wasting reach and attention.

## Responsive, accessibility and system-state proof

The canonical set includes phone compact, short phone landscape, tablet portrait/landscape, Windows compact/wide, dark, 200% mixed high-contrast stress, four-state system comparison and first/mid/end/reverse/reduced-motion evidence. The component manifest retains touch, mouse, keyboard and screen-reader contracts.
