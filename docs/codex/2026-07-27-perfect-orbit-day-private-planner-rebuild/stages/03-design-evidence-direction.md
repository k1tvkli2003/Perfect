# Stage 03 — Design evidence and revolutionary creative direction

Status: pending  
Depends on: Stages 01–02  
Blocks: every visual specification, preview and runtime implementation

## Mission

Define one distinctive Perfect! experience system before touching production UI.
The direction must improve comprehension, repeated-use speed, recoverability,
emotional rhythm and personal ownership—not merely add pastel color, blur and
rounded rectangles.

This stage is concept and evidence work only. It may create project-local design
artifacts, screenshots, diagrams and preview assets; it must not mutate shipped
Flutter UI, routes, domain behavior or database contracts.

## Autonomous design authority

The owner has delegated ordinary product and design decisions. Do not pause for
routine voting. Generate alternatives, challenge them with Modernize, Integrity,
Anatomy, Style and Critics criteria, select the strongest coherent direction and
record why it won. The resulting internally accepted direction is the working spec.

This delegation does not waive evidence: a convenient option cannot win because it
is easier to code. A later material direction change requires a new preview and a
recorded supersession decision before implementation continues.

## Mandatory evidence inventory

1. Freeze current Android phone/tablet and Windows compact/expanded screenshots for
   every reachable route, overlay and native widget size, including empty/sparse/
   dense, light/dark, loading/offline/retry/error/conflict and IME-open states.
2. Inventory every entrypoint, component family, page composition, external surface,
   live data source, mutation and responsive transformation in
   [preview-production-gate.md](preview-production-gate.md).
3. Create the Integrity five-way ledger (`KEEP`, `REFINE`, `REDESIGN`, `REMOVE`,
   `ADD`) for identity, shell, navigation, components, pages, flows, states, motion,
   widget, notifications, feedback, auth, settings and release surfaces.
4. Decompose the accepted Perfect! mark, wordmark, pastel relationships, material,
   negative space and motion personality. Protect the exact chosen identity while
   allowing its platform expressions to adapt.
5. Decompose HabitNow screenshots by decision order, field grouping, recurrence,
   recovery and logging ergonomics—not cosmetic imitation.
6. Research current Android adaptive/large-screen and Windows/Fluent interaction
   conventions from primary sources, plus current planner/task/focus peers. Convert
   observations into local rules; never cargo-cult their identity.
7. Prove production fixture isolation: sample names/tasks seen in preview builds are
   not part of the real signed owner flow.

## Generative direction process

1. Start from product truth: a private, long-session, local-first daily instrument
   used by one owner across Android and Windows.
2. Produce at least 24 raw recipes spanning different mental models, topology,
   interaction, material, state expression and motion—not 24 palette variants.
3. Force at least one radical rebuild, one operationally calm direction, one
   Windows-first workspace composition and one mobile one-handed composition.
4. Reject template dashboards, card piles, decorative orbit metaphors, detached
   bottom bars, huge dead headers, arbitrary glass and visually impressive flows
   that increase taps or hide state.
5. Shortlist 6–8 concepts by UX gain, then 2–4 complete directions.
6. Generate a separate high-fidelity phone + tablet + Windows preview family for
   every finalist using realistic planner content and explicit empty/error states.
7. Score each finalist on orientation time, task/habit logging speed, hierarchy,
   originality, accessibility, resilience, feasibility, asset craft, performance
   and whole-product coherence.
8. Select one direction autonomously; record rejected alternatives and concrete
   reasons so discarded ideas do not silently reappear later.

## Fixed product thesis to carry forward

- Perfect! is a calm, tactile **day instrument**, not a dashboard and not a space/
  orbit novelty. Orbit is removed rather than cosmetically reskinned.
- The signature interaction is one floating, morphing Capture/Plan/AI/Voice
  instrument; modes share one spatial surface and preserve independent drafts.
- Pastel is semantic and restrained. Warm apricot, mint, lavender and graphite
  create orientation and state—not a rainbow wash over every surface.
- Glass is reserved for genuinely floating spatial layers: compact header, footer,
  composer and selected overlays. Repeated rows are not glass cards.
- Detail is view-first. Normal row taps lead to detail/history; Edit is explicit.
- Status and habit logging are tactile, reversible, locally immediate and consistent
  in Today, workspaces, detail, widget, notification/deep-link and AI write review.
- Rich graphic material uses dedicated vector/raster/hybrid production; no flat
  circle, stock gradient, emoji or simplistic painter may impersonate crafted art.
- Mobile, tablet and Windows are different first-class compositions under one
  semantic contract. Desktop is not a stretched phone and tablet is not an awkward
  midpoint.

## Preview requirements for the winning direction

- Product identity plate: mark, selected wordmark, app icon masks, splash, widget
  mark, Windows taskbar/Start/installer, light/dark/monochrome and small-size tests.
- Shell plate: compact header, sync cloud, dual date/time, phone footer, collapsed/
  expanded rail, focus rings, hover/press and resize transformation.
- Workflow plate: Today, Tasks, Plan, Habits, Goals/Projects, Focus, detail, wizard,
  capture modes, AI proposal, settings/recovery, feedback and widget.
- State plate: default, hover, focus, pressed, selected, disabled, loading,
  optimistic, undo, success, warning, syncing, offline, conflict, error and
  destructive confirmation.
- Motion storyboard: shell continuity, page title rise, composer morph, selector,
  status/log receipt, sync, overlay, breakpoint transformation and reduced motion.
- Typography plate: exact Perfect! wordmark image plus live English/Persian/mixed
  corpus, numerals, Gregorian/Jalali dates, long labels and 200% scale.

## Whole-product propagation

The chosen direction must explain every row in
[coverage-ledger.md](coverage-ledger.md). A surface may intentionally use a
platform-native variant, but identity, terminology, domain state and action outcome
must remain equivalent. Every unexplained surface is a stage failure, not future
polish.

## Verification and evidence

- The opinion ledger has no silent rows and names evidence/target/verification.
- Current/reference/finalist contact sheets exist at matching aspect ratios.
- Every finalist demonstrates realistic content density and at least one failure or
  recovery state; poster-only concepts are rejected.
- Structural choice is defended using task frequency, information scent, reach,
  hierarchy, state continuity and platform input—not personal taste.
- The selected direction has explicit asset, typography, responsive, motion,
  accessibility and performance implications.

## Reject the stage if

- “Modern” means blur, gradients, giant type, rounded cards or a generic Dribbble
  dashboard without a better task model.
- Only Today or a few hero surfaces are concepted while auth, settings, editor,
  detail, widget, error/recovery or Windows surfaces remain undefined.
- Phone is stretched into tablet/desktop or desktop is shrunk into a phone list.
- A finalist cannot express dense data, long mixed text, 200% scale, dark mode,
  keyboard use, offline work or reduced motion.
- The winner is selected by implementation convenience rather than product evidence.

## Completion handoff

Freeze the selected direction ID, evidence paths, opinion ledger and supersession
rules. Stage 04 receives the winning system and must produce every component preview
before any production UI implementation. Commit/push the design documents and assets;
do not publish a runtime release when no runtime byte changed.
